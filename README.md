## 👋 About Me

I'm a **PhD in Computer Science** and researcher at the [Universidad Tecnológica de Bolívar](https://www.utb.edu.co), based in **Cartagena, Colombia**. My work sits at the intersection of **Natural Language Processing**, **phonetics**, and **AI-driven software architecture**.

- 🔬 Research focus: **Phonesthemes**, multilingual affective computing, and language models
- 🏛️ Affiliated researcher at **UTB** — registered in Colombia's Scienti/MinCiencias system
- 🌎 Passionate about NLP for **Spanish and low-resource languages**
- 📬 Reach me at **epuertas@utb.edu.co**
